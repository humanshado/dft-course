# DFT Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Density Functional Theory: A Practical Introduction_ (2nd ed., 2022) by Sholl & Steckel, Wiley](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/natural-sciences/density-functional-theory-978-1-119-84086-2)
  The most practical first textbook: what DFT calculations are, convergence, k-points, functional choice, DFT+U, dispersion. Use for: the "how do I actually run this" spine of the course.
- [Book: _Materials Modelling using Density Functional Theory_ (2014) by Feliciano Giustino, OUP](https://tenlong.com.tw/products/9780199662449)
  Graduate-level, unified treatment of structural, electronic, optical and magnetic properties; author is a Quantum ESPRESSO contributor. Use for: PhD-level depth on band structures, phonons, pseudopotentials.
- [Book (free PDF): _The ABC of DFT_ by Kieron Burke & friends (UC Irvine)](https://dft.uci.edu/doc/g1.pdf)
  Clear, theory-first text with pen-and-paper exercises. Use for: Hohenberg–Kohn, Kohn–Sham, exchange–correlation, exact conditions. Newer chapters at [Burke group: Learn DFT](https://www.chem.uci.edu/~kieron/dft/learnDFT.php).
- [Lecture: Walter Kohn, Nobel Lecture "Electronic Structure of Matter – Wave Functions and Density Functionals" (1999)](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1998/kohn/lecture/)
  The founder's own overview; §II.B introduces the "exponential wall". Use for: motivation, history, the big picture.
- [Docs: Quantum ESPRESSO — Learn page](https://www.quantum-espresso.org/learn/) and [Getting started](https://www.quantum-espresso.org/?p=99)
  Official tutorials, user guide, input-variable documentation. Use for: every QE input keyword; installation.
- [Video/slides: Quantum ESPRESSO schools archive (Materials Cloud)](https://www.materialscloud.org/learn/sections/VLoB41/quantum-espresso-schools)
  Recorded lectures and hands-on exercises from official QE schools. Use for: guided hands-on QE practice.
- [Paper: Giannozzi et al., "QUANTUM ESPRESSO: a modular and open-source software project…", J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 21, 395502 (2009)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.2569)
  The code's reference paper. Use for: what QE implements, and what to cite in your thesis.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Matter Modeling Stack Exchange](https://mattermodeling.stackexchange.com/)
  Moderated Q&A for molecular and materials modelling, including QE. Use for: "why is my calculation doing X" questions, methods advice.
- [Quantum ESPRESSO users mailing list](https://lists.quantum-espresso.org/mailman/listinfo)
  Official users list (subscribe to post; searchable archives). Developers answer here. Use for: QE-specific errors and input questions — search the archive first.
